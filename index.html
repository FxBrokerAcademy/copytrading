Basics

What is copy trading?

Copy trading is a form of social trading where the decisions of experienced traders are transparently tracked and—based on your own settings—automatically mirrored to a connected account. It lets newcomers watch real decision processes and make market mechanisms tangible.

The focus is on understanding: strategies, risk parameters, and reactions to market changes become visible and can be analysed.

Transparency & control
  • Learn by observing real strategies
  • Set parameters yourself and adjust anytime
  • Activities are traceable and documented
  • Pause or switch strategies whenever needed
Process

Copy trading in three steps

  1. Select a trader: Choose a trader on a copy-trading platform with a verifiable history and a clear approach.
  2. Connect your account: Link your trading account securely and set individual parameters (e.g., allocation size, max concurrent positions).
  3. Automatic execution: New decisions by the selected trader are mirrored automatically—fully controllable and changeable at any time.

Notice

Risk & learning notice

Trading the financial markets involves risks. Copy trading is not a guarantee of outcomes and should primarily be seen as a learning and observation tool. Do your research carefully and adjust settings responsibly. Past activity is not an indicator of future developments.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is copy trading suitable for beginners?

Yes. It lets you observe decisions of experienced traders in real time and better understand their approach—without doing every analysis yourself.

Do I keep control over my account?

Yes. You set the parameters, can pause, switch strategies, or manually adjust individual positions.

What should I consider when choosing a trader?

Verifiable history, clear strategy, documented risk management, and regular communication of the approach.

What costs may apply?

Depending on the platform, fees may apply (e.g., service or management fees). Review each platform’s terms carefully.
